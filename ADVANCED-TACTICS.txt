Advanced tactics (optional)

F4 > Monster AI > Navigation > Advanced tactics. Default Off.
Independent of Aggressive AI and Persistent pursuit; applies immediately.

Shared: nearby allies give each other space and try different directions.
Imps: sidestep after fireballs.
Demons/spectres: approach from different diagonal angles and separate in crowds.
Cacodemons: drift sideways after shots and vary height with floor/ceiling clearance.
Hell knights: take short advancing steps after launching attacks.
Barons: hold nearby chokepoints and occasionally chain a three-shot barrage.
Revenants: back away at medium-close range while preserving close melee.
Arachnotrons: sustain bursts, then strafe; seek a new angle when their firing lane is blocked.
Mancubi: alternate focused fixed-position volleys and wider spreading volleys.
Arch-viles: approach visible nearby corpses that fit when raised; probe nearby cover and expose themselves again to attack.
Pain elementals: keep distance and try to find room in front before spawning souls.
Cyberdemons: occasionally lead a moving target, capped to a modest 96-unit offset.
Spider masterminds: sustained suppression bursts, briefly aimed at the last visible position, then reposition.

These are local tactics, not guaranteed perfect navigation. All actual steps
use the existing collision checks. Nearby searches and cover probes are capped,
and decisions are staggered. No damage, projectile speed, graphics, demo or
network behavior changes when the option is off. Existing saves remain usable.

Validation: 24-test suite plus targeted native/GPU checks; bounded prediction,
fixed/wide volleys, aerial clearance and off/demo guards. All three AI options
were exercised together in the user's AITEST E1M5 save: the imp found its way
around the obstacle and attacked. The isolated monster simulation measured
0.009 ms average and 0.339 ms maximum per tic in that scene (not whole-frame FPS).

Lost soul recovery: advanced tactics now includes lost souls (which do not count toward kills). Blocked charges against scenery or other actors trigger a two-second repositioning period, respected by Aggressive AI. Enabling Advanced tactics displays a warning about difficulty, especially for pistol starts.
