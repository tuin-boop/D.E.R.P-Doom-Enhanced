Tuin-Doom-Enhanced - quieter notices

- Close call, barrel chain, three kills in one attack, and tough fist-kill awards now appear only once per map per player, including across save/load. Entering a new map resets them.
- Secret discoveries simply say "Found a secret area!", with no extra slogan. Each newly found secret still gets its notification.
- Retains the previous fracture effects and performance fixes, 250% destruction damage default, disabled extra death animations, and rarer Toasty (2%). Existing profiles retain their saved settings.

Validation: native notice regressions (save/load, map reset, disabled notices), gameplay, firing, save/load and level-change checks passed.

Download Tuin-Doom-Enhanced.exe and select your own IWAD. Commercial game data is not included. Previous releases remain available.

Based on Duct-Tape DOOM by Robert Heidenreich: https://github.com/robongrui/duct-tape-doom. Original Doom by id Software. Existing credits and licenses are retained.
