Tuin-Doom-Enhanced — door performance and impact fixes

Download Tuin-Doom-Enhanced.exe and select your own Doom IWAD. Commercial game data is not included.

Changes since October 20:
- Reuse unchanged crater geometry and GPU buffers to reduce damaged-door overhead.
- Improve local collision caching and geometry processing around accumulated destruction.
- Spread costly monster pursuit searches across updates to reduce stutters around damaged geometry.
- Check bullet height before breaking glass; shots below or above a pane leave it intact.
- Prevent cosmetic bullet puffs from snapping to original sector floors/ceilings.
- Apply the new impact effects to excavated surfaces, suppressing duplicate classic puffs when enabled, including with wall damage disabled.

Retains the previous destruction features, settings, HUD, and bundled mods. Existing save/settings folders remain compatible. Older releases are preserved.

Validation: saved-game door opening/closing and plasma replays, retained-geometry comparisons, pursuit guards, glass-height and impact-toggle regression checks. Performance improvements are scene-dependent; this is not a guarantee of 10 ms frame times.

Based on Duct-Tape DOOM, the engine and renderer by Robert Heidenreich: https://github.com/robongrui/duct-tape-doom. Original Doom by id Software. Existing third-party credits and licenses are retained.
