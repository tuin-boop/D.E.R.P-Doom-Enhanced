ADAPTIVE EMISSION AND FASTER BAKING - 2026-10-15

The engine measures emissive texel coverage over the actual wall, floor and
ceiling area of each map. Above 8% coverage in a fixture's hue, its emission
is reduced smoothly according to that coverage. This is automatic: there
are no SIGIL names, map numbers or special-case texture dimensions in the
calculation. Repeating a small texture over a large area counts correctly.
Ordinary sparse fixtures remain at their original strength. Red-dominated
coverage does not attenuate an isolated blue fixture. The adjustment affects
the material glow, bloom and its baked/live light sources consistently.

Lava and glowing slime keep their visible self-glow. Their emitted light
retains at least 40% of its original source strength. A separate local RGB
shoulder limits excessive accumulated lamp light, including its live bump
and gloss contributions, while keeping transient weapon lights separate.
Texture artwork, renderer features and the user's graphics settings remain.

Measured coverage: SIGIL E5M1 RGB 23.6%, 4.7%, 1.4%; Doom E1M1 about 0.8%
per channel, E1M2 0.8%, 0.7%, 0.6%. E1M1/E1M2 therefore do not activate the
material coverage reduction. This coverage estimate is map-wide; the RGB
accumulation limit is evaluated locally. It is not camera auto-exposure.

Loading optimization: a balanced XY bounding tree skips linedefs a bake ray
cannot reach. The exact intersection, masks, sector heights, decoration
occlusion and all lighting sample counts are retained. Door/lift movement
does not invalidate these XY bounds. Equal-distance intersections preserve
their original order. Small sectors keep the simple linear search.

SIGIL E5M1 previously took about 25-30 seconds in the profiler on this PC;
the optimized runs were typically 8-10 seconds (9.5 seconds in the final
visual check). Timing varies with other work on the computer. This is a
loading improvement, not a promise of higher in-game FPS.

Developer verification: -verifybakerays compares accelerated and linear ray
results, including exact hit coordinates. A complete SIGIL load and sampled
views passed this comparison. The baked-lighting unit test also compares
12,000 rays through a concave 160-sided room and checks coverage attenuation,
blue fixture preservation, liquid illumination and monotonic light rolloff.
