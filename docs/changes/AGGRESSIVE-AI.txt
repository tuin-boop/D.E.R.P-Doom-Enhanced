Aggressive AI (optional)

F4 > Monster AI > Navigation > Aggressive AI.
Default: Off. Independent of Persistent pursuit; applies immediately.

Alerted monsters attack whenever they have a clear firing line instead of
waiting for the usual random attack chance. Their existing attacks, damage,
projectile speeds and collision rules remain intact.

Pistol enemies use the player's 14-tic pistol cycle (10 with Fluid fast pistol).
Shotgun enemies use the player's 37-tic pump cycle.
Hitscan misses and missed projectile impacts request a short repositioning
burst. Successful hits permit continued firing, with occasional short strafes.
Demons and spectres occasionally approach diagonally to try to flank.
Heavy hits (40+ damage) briefly stagger surviving monsters.
Every third eligible mancubus attack sequence starts a short barrage aimed at
a fixed last-known position. It can continue after the player leaves that spot,
but expires after 160 tics and respects death/notarget.

Original AI remains available with the toggle off. Demos and network games
keep original behavior. No save format changes; transient tactics rebuild on
load. Actor and projectile tracking are bounded.

Validation includes live pistol/shotgun cadence, projectile hit/miss feedback,
heavy-hit stagger, fixed-position barrage/expiry, live toggles and demo guards.

Enabling Aggressive AI displays a difficulty warning: it can make the game hard, and very hard with pistol starts. The same warning applies to Advanced tactics.
