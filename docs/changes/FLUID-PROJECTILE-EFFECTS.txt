FluidWeapons impacts and projectile effects - 2026-10-18

Uses the original FluidWeapons decal definitions: five BulletChip variants for pistol, shotguns, chaingun and chainsaw; Scorch for rockets; purple plasma scorch over a black base; green BFG lightning over the BFG scorch. These are real decal masks, not Doom puff silhouettes. Fists have no weapon decal in the source mod.

Projectile visuals are adapted from FluidWeapons Player.zsc for Doom's rocket, plasma and BFG weapons: rocket smoke/exhaust/sparks and blast smoke; plasma trail, flying particles, rising/falling sparks, electrical arcs and steam; BFG orbiting particles, trail, impact arcs/burst, and hit-target steam/sparks/traces. Original projectile/explosion actors and gameplay damage remain in place. Particles use batched textured quads with additive glow and bounded lifetime/count; they are cosmetic and do not consume gameplay random numbers.

The bundled FluidWeapons toggle controls these marks and projectile effects. The separately selected Stewie Uzi gun impact/puff remains under F4 > Effects > Gun impacts.

Single-player free-look now aims rockets, plasma and BFG projectiles vertically at the crosshair and preserves total projectile speed. Classic demo/network aiming is retained.

Asset provenance and source definitions are in assets/fluid-effects. Common decals referenced by FluidWeapons are inherited from the base engine; their artwork was imported from the local UZDoom package. Particle masks are from the supplied FluidWeapons package.
