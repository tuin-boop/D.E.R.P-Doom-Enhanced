FRAME-TIME SPIKE UPDATE - 16 October 2026

Launch DuctTapeDoom-smooth.exe (or the updated DuctTapeDoom.exe).
Renderer, resolution, graphics settings and visual effects are unchanged.

CHANGES
1. Optional -profilespikes tracing records slow-frame time spent outside the
   renderer, acquiring the swapchain, preparing the scene, uploading, drawing
   and submitting. Geometry timing separates wall/floor work, surface lights,
   actors and effects. No GPU fences are added; diagnostics are off normally.
2. Exact shadow-slice packing is reused when position/radius/cone and the
   complete ordered blocker geometry match. Strength and flicker remain live.
   Changed blockers/light shapes rebuild. The existing -verifylightthreads
   mode forces full recomputation and compares packed output bit-for-bit.
3. Blocker cache capacity now evicts only the least recently used entry,
   retaining its allocation. Previously moving projectiles could fill the
   cache and flush ALL stationary lights as well. Sector geometry changes
   still invalidate it. -verifylightcache compares with fresh wall scans.
4. Reuse actor-light jobs, caster lists, sky-height and reflection scratch.
   Merge visibility spans in place and reuse shadow polygon clipping buffers,
   retaining the original calculations and ordering. Four-point footprints
   now live on the stack. Light-mask job storage also retains capacity.

TEST METHOD
Tech Gone Bad e1m8b.wad at 2560x1440, existing full graphics settings,
Fluid Weapons 1.0-UZ + sound addon + FluidMonsters, UZ movement/interpolation.
Core i7-14700F / RTX 5070. Active monsters and held plasma fire in the same
previously slow view. Normal GPU pipelining and tear-free mailbox presentation;
no per-frame GPU fences. Audio uses the test driver's silent output backend.
This is a repeatable stationary combat test, not a complete playthrough.

RESULTS (same-session 60-second comparison)
Previous build: 126.3 FPS, p99 14.50 ms, worst 24.83 ms;
54 of 7581 frames exceeded 16.67 ms (0.71%).
Updated build: 136.7 FPS, p99 13.52 ms, worst 22.20 ms;
39 of 8203 frames exceeded 16.67 ms (0.48%).

42 of 8283 frames exceeded 16.67 ms (0.51%).

The paired run has approximately 28% fewer over-budget frames and 8% higher
average FPS. Spikes remain; this is not a guaranteed 60 FPS minimum. Results
vary with background activity. The earlier 30-second intermediate cache-only
build did not consistently reduce spike counts; the final build also includes
the bounded LRU blocker cache. Performance runs have no verification enabled.
