Impact effects and accessible Effects tab - 18 October 2026

F4 > Effects (next to Display)
- Doors: Opening door sparks; Rocket-breachable doors.
- Combat: Gun impacts; Rocket impacts; existing blood and shootable lights.

Gun impacts (default On): bullet marks, a short bright spark burst and smaller
surface-colored chips on walls, floors and ceilings. Turning it off retains
original bullet puffs. Liquid splashes, blood and shootable lights retain
separate controls.

Rocket impacts (default On): a bounded burst of embers and surface-colored
debris, with scorch marks on nearby walls or floors. Turning it off retains
the original rocket explosion. Neither option changes weapon damage/ammo.
New particles use simulation timestamps, their own RNG and a shared cap.

The previous instructions incorrectly called Atmosphere > Doors 'Effects'.
Effects is now an actual tab, visible immediately beside Display.

Validation: all 24 tests passed, including option registration/config loading,
disabled/enabled effects and particle budgets. Fullscreen 2560x1440 captures
checked bullet impacts, rocket explosion and the final Effects menu layout.
