Impact rework - 2026-10-18

F4 > Effects contains Opening door sparks, Gun impacts and Rocket impacts.
Door breaching has been withdrawn from gameplay and the menu. Opening sparks remain cosmetic. Legacy save fields are retained for compatibility.

Gun impacts now use tapered sparks, rotating material chips and soft dust, replacing the stock surface-hit puff when enabled.
Rocket impacts use flame, smoke, sparks and fragments. Particle movement respects solid walls, closed doors, floors and ceilings and can pass through open portals. This is a bounded particle effect, not a fluid simulation.
Both impact options can be disabled to restore the stock effects. Gameplay damage is unchanged.

Validation: all 24 automated tests passed; native and GPU gameplay/save-load tests passed again after the final changes. Fullscreen impact and Effects-menu captures reviewed.
