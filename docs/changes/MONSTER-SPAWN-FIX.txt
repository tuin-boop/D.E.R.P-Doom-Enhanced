FLUID MONSTER SPAWN FIX - 16 October 2026

Launch DuctTapeDoom-monsters.exe or the updated DuctTapeDoom.exe.

Some vanilla maps place monsters slightly overlapping a blocking line.
An ordinary Doom walking step can clear that overlap, but the smaller Fluid
substeps all remain inside it and fail. The E1M7 STUCK save reproduced this
with six corridor monsters at y=-400, still at their exact spawn positions.
Their direction was DI_NODIR while their chase counters kept decreasing.

The fix checks whether the current position overlaps an obstacle at chase
state entry. In that case only, the original collision-checked full Doom step
runs for that state, with no additional substeps. Once clear, smooth movement
resumes. No collision bypass or teleport is used. Existing saves recover.
Demo playback, recording and network modes retain their existing behavior.
Renderer, graphics and previous performance/water/audio fixes are retained.

VALIDATION
Loaded a copy of the user's STUCK save in fullscreen 2560x1440 and observed
10 seconds with the same player position and no player attacks:
- Previous Fluid movement: all six remained at their original corridor positions.
- Fluid disabled: the same monsters immediately moved clear.
- Updated Fluid movement: all six moved clear and resumed their normal chase.
The user also confirmed that the observed test appeared fixed.
