Original Fluid Weapons switching

Replaces the generic whole-weapon tilt with per-tick original Select/Deselect
poses for all nine weapons: frame changes, support hands, pivots, scale,
rotation, mirrors, and alternate draws/holsters. Includes separate gloved-fist
sequences. The native playback does not load a scripting runtime.

The engine still owns ammo and firing timing. Firing/auxiliary actions cancel
visual settling, and chaingun rotation transfers back to normal playback.
Ordinary switching is covered; special mid-fire lower/reload branches and the
optional chainsaw starter-cord mode are not included in these clips.

Validation: fullscreen 2560x1440 captures of all nine weapons, every captured
sprite composed successfully, reproducible generated data, 24/24 tests passed.

Smooth switching correction: continuous captured poses now interpolate at
the display frame rate even when the original PSprite interpolation flag
was cleared. Animation duration and frame order are unchanged. A regression
test checks the intermediate shotgun poses; all three relevant suites pass.
