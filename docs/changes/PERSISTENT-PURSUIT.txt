Persistent pursuit (optional)

F4 > Monster AI > Navigation > Persistent pursuit.
Default: Off. Applies immediately and is stored with graphics settings.

Alerted monsters without line of sight can search short routes around walls,
follow the resulting waypoints, and retry blocked routes. It does not wake
unaware monsters or change their attacks. Actual movement still uses Doom's
collision, step, headroom and drop-off checks. Demos and network games keep
the original chase behavior.

Navigation is local, not a guarantee of solving every map. Searches are
incremental (24 node expansions per tic globally, 160 per search), with
bounded route storage. Routes reset on level changes and save loading.

Validation: compared the user's AITEST E1M5 save with pursuit disabled/enabled.
Disabled: the imp paced behind the wall. Enabled: it went around the passage.
Incremental simulation measured 0.007 ms mean / 0.322 ms maximum per monster
simulation tic in this scene; this is not a whole-frame FPS measurement.
