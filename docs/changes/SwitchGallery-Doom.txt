SWITCH GALLERY: 58 alcoves, both states of every SW1/SW2 texture in DOOM.WAD.
Start a new game at E1M1. Walk forward into each alcove and press Use at its back panel.
Buttons change state for one second, then reset. Adjacent alcoves show the paired starting states.
Eight alcoves per row, connected by open corridors. No monsters, hazards or moving doors.
Neutral walls and dim fixed lighting make the switch glow easier to compare.
The CSV lists panel names and entrance coordinates; the engine displays the room label.
Textures without lamps intentionally remain unlit. No IWAD artwork is included.
