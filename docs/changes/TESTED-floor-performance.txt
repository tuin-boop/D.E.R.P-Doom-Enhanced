2026-10-19 floor/ceiling performance and liquid exclusion

Sparse subdivision keeps four-unit detail near craters and coarse polygons in
undamaged space, rather than tessellating the bounding rectangle between holes.
Zero-area clipped triangles are discarded. Animated floor textures and reflection
flags update without rebuilding geometry. Distant actors skip crater collision
refresh; depth sampling stops once an unaffected support point is found.

Stress test: 120 pairs of floor/ceiling impacts across E1M1. Before changes the
flat geometry CPU stage averaged 23.184 ms across 128 frames. With the optimizations
and before liquid exclusion it averaged 2.660 ms in the first window and 1.969 ms
in the following steady window. These are CPU-stage timings, not total frame time.
Texture-animation rebuilds stopped (23 repeated builds of some leaves before).
Logs: test-surfaces/floor-stress-before.log and floor-stress-final.log.

Liquid families NUKAGE/FWATER/SLIME/BLOOD/LAVA and sky are excluded from flat
excavation. Old liquid craters are suppressed in rendering/collision too.
