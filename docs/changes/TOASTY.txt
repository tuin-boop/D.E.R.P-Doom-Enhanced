TOASTY GIB EASTER EGG
A monster's actual extreme-death (gib) transition has a 10% cosmetic chance.
Ordinary deaths and player deaths do not trigger it. Cosmetic random rolls do
not consume Doom's gameplay RNG. Concurrent triggers do not stack voices or
keep the picture onscreen.

Uses the supplied full-shoulders transparent portrait and Toasty voice clip.
The original 1190x1322 PNG is embedded unchanged and drawn in its own GPU pass
at display resolution, after world postprocessing, outside the 320x200 HUD.
The portrait rises from the bottom-right over 0.18 seconds, stays until 0.90,
then slides down over 0.16 seconds. Animation follows game time and pauses.
Audio uses the effects-volume setting. Both assets are embedded in the engine;
no external image, video, codec or sound mod is needed at runtime.

Asset provenance: portrait supplied in this chat; voice supplied at
the supplied Toasty WEBM clip.
The voice is trimmed from 0.10 to 1.00 seconds to remove surrounding silence,
and embedded as 22050 Hz mono PCM. assets/toasty/embed.py regenerates headers.
These supplied media assets are separate from the engine's source-code license.

Validation: fullscreen 2560x1440 visual check of the high-resolution overlay.
100 ordinary deaths produced no event; 1000 gibs produced 109 events.
Audio mixer accepted the embedded voice (19845 samples at 22050 Hz).
Exit/disappearance frames checked. Defender scan clean; embedded engine hash verified.
