This update adds optional monster AI modes and revised weapon impacts, plus the latest lost-soul recovery and difficulty warnings.

- **Persistent pursuit:** bounded local navigation around obstacles.
- **Aggressive AI:** sustained clear-shot attacks, faster gunfire, repositioning after misses, and occasional fixed-position Mancubus barrages.
- **Advanced tactics:** species-specific flanking, sidestepping, crowd separation, cover, suppression, and limited predictive attacks.
- Both Aggressive AI and Advanced tactics warn that they can make the game hard, especially with pistol starts. All three AI options default off and are available under **F4 > Monster AI**.
- Lost souls blocked by scenery now get a two-second repositioning period before another charge, including when Aggressive AI is enabled.
- Improved escape from existing overlap at lowered lifts. The separately reported imp near a lift has not been reproduced without its save.
- Optional Uzi-style gun impacts from the supplied Stewie-Brian-Arsenal assets, including animated puff/sparks and voxel debris.
- FluidWeapons scorch marks and projectile effects, with wall-edge clipping and vertical free-look projectile aiming in single player.
- Cosmetic opening-door sparks. Experimental door breaching and the custom rocket-explosion option were withdrawn; the original rocket explosion remains.

Validation: the gameplay update passed all 24 automated tests. The subsequent Aggressive AI warning change builds successfully. Source matches the local workspace, and both ZIP archives passed integrity checks.
