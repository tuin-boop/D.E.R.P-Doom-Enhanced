Uzi impacts - 2026-10-18

Gun impacts (F4 > Effects) now use the supplied Stewie-Brian-Arsenal v0.233 Uzi impact art: four animated sparks, the 16-frame flash/puff sequence, and the five animated KVX voxel dust models. Exposed voxel faces and sprite runs are embedded in the engine; the external mod is not required. Cosmetic timing and movement are adapted to this renderer and use a separate random stream. A bounded pool limits rapid-fire effect accumulation.

Turning Gun impacts off restores stock bullet puffs. Flesh hits keep the existing blood path.

The custom rocket explosion and its menu option have been removed. Rockets use the original explosion again. Opening door sparks remain; door breaching remains withdrawn.

Asset provenance is retained in assets/uzi-impacts/credits.txt. tools/import_uzi_impacts.py can regenerate the embedded art from the supplied PK3. This imports only the impact assets, not the rest of the mod.
