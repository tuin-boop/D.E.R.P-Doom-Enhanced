WATER AND POSITIONAL AUDIO UPDATE - 16 October 2026

Launch DuctTapeDoom-water.exe (or the updated DuctTapeDoom.exe).
The Duct Tape Doom renderer and previous door-performance work are retained.

CHANGES
- Positional sounds now attenuate normally on map 8. The old Doom exception
  used a minimum volume of 15, which is full volume in this engine's range.
  Deliberately global boss sounds retain their existing non-positional calls.
- Sky fog keeps atmospheric haze but excludes local light-volume glow, so
  nearby animated/pulsing lights cannot tint the distant sky through this path.
  Sky alpha is explicitly opaque outside the reflection capture, preventing
  texture coverage from being interpreted as heat-haze strength.
- Liquid detail becomes calmer with distance (192 to 1024 map units): palette
  mip sampling and gentler bump highlights stabilize distant waves. This also
  works with global filtering disabled. Nearby animation remains unchanged;
  water, nukage, slime, blood and lava retain their textures and animation.
- Water mirror distortion is bounded to 2.5 reference pixels and diminishes
  with distance. Reflection edges fade instead of stretching border samples.
  Reflection color and proximity now sample the same distorted location.
- Settled corpse shadows use a low visual height and shorter footprint rather
  than projecting their collision height into long strips from low lights.
  This addresses a plausible source of the dark patches in the supplied shots.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS
Fullscreen 2560x1440 runtime checks with the user's graphics settings, base
Ultimate Doom IWAD, Tech Gone Bad, Fluid Weapons, SFX addon and FluidMonsters.
A 152-view map sweep passed on the initial version; the final liquid sampling
was checked in the outdoor pool view (-1560,48 facing east), with screenshot
inspection and shader compilation. This view retained nearby liquid detail.
The profiler waits for the GPU; screenshot FPS text includes capture stalls
and is not a live gameplay performance result.
A runtime regression check compares positional sound falloff on maps 1 and 8
at 21 distances, checks intermediate attenuation and far-distance silence.
The exact corpse arrangement and moving-camera artifacts from the user's
screenshots were not reproduced, so those visual fixes still need confirmation
in that saved scene. No claim is made that every reported artifact is resolved.

All 23 CTest checks passed. Embedded engine matches the tested Release engine.
Microsoft Defender found no threats.
