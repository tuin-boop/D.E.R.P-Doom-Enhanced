BUNDLED MODS
FluidWeapons 1.0-UZ, FluidWeapons SFX add-on and FluidMonsters are embedded in this launcher. No external copies are required. Existing matching packs in the add-on list are skipped; other WADs and PK3s remain as before.
In game: F4 > Bundled mods. Toggle FluidWeapons, FluidWeapons SFX add-on and FluidMonsters independently. Close the options panel to apply. Choices persist in graphics.cfg; all three default to enabled. No level restart required.
Weapon off restores stock weapon presentation and firing behavior. SFX off restores main-pack sounds when FluidWeapons is on; with both off, stock weapon sounds are used. Monster off restores vanilla stepping. Demo/netgame restrictions remain. The embedded FluidMonsters archive is preserved, while the engine uses its existing native adaptation rather than executing ZScript.
Bundled original archives and their credits are preserved. Main weapons/SFX: MR's FluidWeapons packs supplied by the user. FluidMonsters: supplied FluidMonsters package. Original notices/license files remain inside each archive.
Verification: 24/24 automated tests; extracted embedded-pack gameplay/save/load; all eight live toggle combinations and base/add-on sound decode; embedded hashes and duplicate filtering; packaged-engine fullscreen render with all mods disabled and persisted settings.
Also includes LIQUID-CALM.txt changes. Fullscreen E1M1 median 1.4 ms before/after; E1M3 median 1.8 ms before/after (95th 1.9/2.0 ms). Controlled static views, not a gameplay FPS guarantee.
