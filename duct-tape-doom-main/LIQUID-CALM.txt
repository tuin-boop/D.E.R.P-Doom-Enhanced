LIQUID LIGHT AND DISTANT WATER
Adapted two focused ideas from DERP 0.5.0 (Robert Heidenreich): limit pool-area lighting on vertical walls to half the source strength, and reduce distant animated-liquid contrast/pulsing toward its animation-cycle average. Existing reflection bounds, distance damping, lighting overload controls and multicore baking remain.
The water implementation caches averages for every animation cycle per level and adds no texture lookups, particles or procedural noise. Nearby artwork is unchanged; distant texture detail retains 35% contrast, blended gradually with distance and grazing angle.
24/24 automated tests passed, including wall illumination cap and unchanged horizontal illumination. Matched fullscreen E1M1 checks at 2560x1440 reported 1.4 ms median before and after; these are controlled views, not a gameplay FPS guarantee.
Tech Gone Bad could not be retested because the earlier e1m8b.wad path is missing. The available SIGIL II episode is outside the existing performance harness map enumeration; no SIGIL performance claim is made.
